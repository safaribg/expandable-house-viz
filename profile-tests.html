Profile tests — M · L · V · S · SV

Everything from the 2026-08-10 session in one place: Ivan's M, L, R(=M), S and the proposed V and SV, under the three load cases. Drag to orbit.

Results

The five findings of the day:
1 · A divider beats thicker walls for wall buckling — L beats M 3.3× in braced compression at equal weight.
2 · No section dominates another in everything: bending wants caps, torsion wants uniform thick walls, compression wants subdivision. Checked ~3 000 candidates per envelope.
3 · Tall free-standing columns fail by whole-column (Euler) buckling — the 70×35 family manages only ~8 kN at 2.7 m; the square S reaches 44 kN. Cell patterns barely help there.
4 · Horizontal beams are deflection-limited, and stiffness is envelope-locked — bonded-to-panel horizontals are fine, free spans >1.5 m are not.
5 · S's beam weakness is its 62 mm flange (218 MPa): SV's cross fixes it — 2.7× beam strength for +17 % weight, with column capacity almost unchanged.

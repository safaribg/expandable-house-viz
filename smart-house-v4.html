SMART HOUSE V4 — facade & panel installation

Built to Ivan’s dimensioned elevation: 7 449 × 6 058 mm, 2 706 mm high, 2 550 mm clear. Glass 837.9 / 819.1 / 798 × 2 314. It arrives folded at 2 506 mm and unfolds to 7 449. The wall is 3 mm FRP + 70 mm profile + 3 mm FRP = 76 mm — that IS the construction, not a shell over it. The 60 × 120 cm × 30 mm PU stone and the 30 mm larch panel are then glued flat onto the outer skin — no sub-frame, no cavity, 30 mm build-out. Three full 1 200 courses to +3 600, so the glazing head sits 226 mm above two courses and the third course hides the solar 894 mm clear of the roof. Front and rear gables are identical.
